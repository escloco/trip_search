# Milos — Backup 🥈 (strongest)

Vetted against the brief: 4–5h from Athens, bars-but-not-clubs, one house for 6, affordable private
skipper day, €2,000–3,000 all-in. **Best all-round backup if Paros has no house for 6.**

## Getting there
- **SeaJets fast ferry** Piraeus → Milos, **~2h40m** (well inside window); fastest ~2h35m.
- Prices ~**€41–79 pp** each way; ~27 weekly crossings June–Sept.
- Operators: SeaJets, Fast Ferries, ANEK/Minoan.
- Link: https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/athens-milos

## Vibe / bars
- Chill bar scene in **Adamantas** (port — bars, food) and **Pollonia** (quieter); not clubby.
- Jaw-dropping scenery: **Kleftiko** white cliffs, **Sarakiniko** lunar coast.

## Private skipper day 🛥️
- Full-day private boat to **Kleftiko** sea caves (+ south coast), boats for up to 8 — affordable,
  stays within the €600–800 line.
- A tender gets you right into the caves; many packages include snacks/drinks.
- Link: https://www.clickandboat.com/us/rentals/country-greece/islands-cyclades/island-milos

## Accommodation (sleeps 6 / 3 bedrooms)
- Good supply of villas/houses for 6; base in **Adamantas** (walkable to bars/food) or **Pollonia**.
- Link: https://www.booking.com/region/gr/milos.html

## Verdict
Closest to the Paros feel — short ferry, dramatic coastline, working private-boat economics, plentiful
housing. **My #1 backup.**
