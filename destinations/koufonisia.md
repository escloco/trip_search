# Koufonisia — Backup (your shortlist pick)

The most **chill** option — gorgeous beaches, no cars. Trade-off: thin nightlife. Works as a backup if
you lean "beaches & relaxed" over "bars."

## Getting there ⚠️
- Fastest ~**4h** from Piraeus (SeaJets / Blue Star / Hellenic Seaways), ~**€45–60 pp**.
- **Only 1–3 sailings/day** — confirm a **Fri 17 out / Sun 19 back** fast ferry exists before
  committing; this is the main risk.
- Link: https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/athens-koufonisia

## Vibe / bars
- Most chill on the list: beaches **Pori, Fanos, Italida**; **no cars** (footpaths + bikes).
- Trade-off: **thin nightlife** — a few beach/harbor bars, nothing like Naoussa.
- Small island — can feel crowded in peak season despite its size.

## Private skipper day 🛥️
- ✅ Affordable — RIB/speedboat with captain to **Keros, Kato Koufonisi, Schinoussa, Iraklia**.
- Operators (e.g. Koufonisia Tours) run 6–7m RIBs + a 10m speedboat; Paros-level economics.
- Links:
  - Koufonisia Tours — https://koufonissiatours.gr/activities-tours/
  - Day trips (CheckYeti) — https://www.checkyeti.com/en/boat-tours/greece/kato-koufonisi/day-trips

## Accommodation (sleeps 6 / 3 bedrooms) ⚠️
- Available but **limited** — fewer big houses than Paros, books out fast.
- **Koufonisi Villas** complex — 4BR & 6BR houses near Pori beach.
- **Villa Elopia / Villa Erossa** — 6BR sister villas at Pori Bay.
- Link: https://rentagreekvilla.com/destinations/koufonisia/

## Verdict
Best **beaches-and-relaxed** vibe and the boat day works — but accept quieter nightlife, and **lock the
ferry + house early** (low ferry frequency + limited large houses).
