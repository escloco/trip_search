# Paros — 1st Choice 🥇

Bachelor party, 6 people, from Athens, **Fri 17 → Sun 19 July 2026** (2 nights). Base: **Naoussa**.

## Why Paros wins
- Sits exactly in the **4–5h-from-Athens** window via fast ferry.
- Relaxed-but-social vibe: beaches by day, **harbor bars + tavernas** by night (not a big-club scene).
- **Affordable private skipper day** (€500–900) — the key economic edge over Naxos (~€1,450/boat).
- **Naoussa** is a charming old harbor town: walkable, great food, stylish bars.

## Getting there
- **SeaJets fast ferry**, Piraeus → Paros, **~2h40m–3h**, ~€60–70 pp each way.
- Peak July (esp. a Fri-out / Sun-back weekend) sells out — book early.
- Fallback: conventional **Blue Star** ferry one-way (~€40 pp, ~5h) to save ~€150–200.
- Links:
  - Ferryhopper — https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/athens-piraeus-to-paros
  - Athens→Paros 2026 guide — https://www.go-ferry.com/trends-and-insights/athens-paros-ultimate-guide

## Private skipper day (the centerpiece) 🛥️
- Full-day private RIB/speedboat for 6, fuel included, **€600–800**.
- Route: **Paros → Antiparos → "Blue Lagoon" (Panteronisi) → Despotiko → sea caves**; swim/snorkel stops + lunch onboard or a beach taverna.
- Depart Naoussa or Pounda; return late afternoon.
- Links:
  - Full-day private RIB cruise — https://zastours.com/full-day-private-cruise-from-paros-on-a-rib-explore-paros-antiparos-and-despotiko
  - Click&Boat — https://www.clickandboat.com/us/rentals/country-greece/islands-cyclades/island-paros/city-paros
  - SamBoat — https://www.samboat.com/boat-rental/paros-greece

## Accommodation — 3 recommendations (3BR / sleeps 6)

**How I searched:** web searches for `Naoussa Paros 3 bedroom villa sleeps 6 private pool` and
`Naoussa house 6 guests walk to town`, then name-verified candidates on Rentavilla / Booking.com /
Airbnb / VRBO / owner sites. Exact 17–19 July prices need manual date entry (platforms block automated
quotes); figures are estimates from listed/avg rates + peak-July premium.

> **Budget reality:** in peak July a 3BR private-pool villa in Naoussa runs ~€400–650/night (weekend
> avg ~€420). To hit the €300–400/night target, take an in-town house without a pool, or a villa
> slightly outside center + ATVs.
