## 1. Seafront Villa — Airbnb (4-villa estate)
🔗 https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/43601632

- **3 bedrooms / 2 bathrooms**: ground-floor master (queen) + 2 basement queens.
- Sea-view terrace with dining + lounge; open-plan equipped kitchen.
- One of 4 villas sharing garden & BBQ; **24h concierge**, free private parking, free wifi.
- **Free port transfer on request**; optional grocery service.
- **Price (17–19 Jul):** €652.16/night → **€1,304.32** total.

## 2. Paradise Resort — Booking
🔗 https://www.booking.com/hotel/gr/paradise-resort-koufonisia.html · official: https://www.paradisekoufonisi.gr/

- Hotel rooms, **21 m²**, balcony, **sea view**, A/C, private bathroom.
- Plan: **3 rooms = 6 people** (2 per room). ~200 m from the port.
- **Price (17–19 Jul):** **€1,590** total for 2 nights / 6 adults (≈ €795/night).

## 3. Aeolos Hotel — Agoda
🔗 https://www.agoda.com/aeolos-hotel/hotel/all/koufonisia-gr.html · official: https://www.aeoloshotel.com/

- Small hotel (24 rooms), **200 m from the port**, pool & sea-view rooms.
- Each room deal covers up to **3 adults** → would need ~2–3 rooms for the group.
- **Price (17–19 Jul):** from **€379/night** (cheapest room shown this session).

> Note: on Koufonisia the group splits across hotel rooms (Paradise / Aeolos) unless the
