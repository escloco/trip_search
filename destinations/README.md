# Destination Research

Per-island research for the Paros bachelor-party trip (6 people, from Athens,
**Fri 17 → Sun 19 July 2026**, 2 nights). Brief: 4–5h from Athens, bars-but-not-clubs,
one house for all 6 (~2 per room), one affordable private skipper day, €2,000–3,000 all-in.

**Fit ranking:** Paros → Milos → Sifnos → Koufonisia → Naxos.

| Destination | Ferry (fast) | Private skipper | House for 6 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Paros](./paros.md) 🥇 | ~2h40m–3h | ✅ €600–800 | Good supply | 1st choice — best all-round fit |
| [Milos](./milos.md) 🥈 | ~2h40m | ✅ affordable (Kleftiko) | Plenty | Strongest backup |
| [Sifnos](./sifnos.md) 🥉 | ~2h30m | ✅ available | Available | Best bars + food |
| [Koufonisia](./koufonisia.md) | ~4h (1–3/day ⚠️) | ✅ affordable | Limited ⚠️ | Most chill; thin nightlife |
| [Naxos](./naxos.md) | ~3.5–4h | ⚠️ from ~€1,450 | Easiest | Only with a shared boat |

See [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) for the full itinerary, budget, and booking logistics.
